Reconstruction — live inverse FFT

What's happening: your image is resampled to N×N, each RGB channel is run through a 2D FFT (in WebAssembly), and the log-magnitude spectrum is shown fftshifted — low frequencies in the middle, high frequencies at the edges. Both left panels are paintable and the other side reacts live. On the image: draw, brighten, darken, or stamp text/emoji and watch its spectrum change (sharp edges spray energy across all frequencies). On the spectrum: attenuate multiplies magnitudes toward zero, boost amplifies them, draw / text / emoji add new magnitude (each painted bin becomes a sinusoidal grating in the image), and the phase tab rotates phase. The right panel is the live inverse FFT of the edited spectrum of the edited image, and difference shows only what the frequency-domain edits changed.

Try: attenuate the outer ring (low-pass blur) · attenuate the center (edge filter) · stamp a tiny faint dot far from center, then crank difference — that's a frequency-domain watermark · write text in the spectrum and see the grating texture it hides in the image · stamp text on the image and find its energy signature in the spectrum · scribble on phase and watch the picture smear while its spectrum barely changes.
