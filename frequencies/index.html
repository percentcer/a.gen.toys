Reconstruction — live inverse FFT

What's happening: your image is resampled to N×N, each RGB channel is run through a 2D FFT (in WebAssembly), and the log-magnitude spectrum is shown fftshifted — low frequencies in the middle, high frequencies at the edges. Anything you paint edits the spectrum spatially: attenuate multiplies magnitudes toward zero, boost amplifies them, draw / text / emoji add new magnitude (each painted bin becomes a sinusoidal grating in the image), and the phase tab rotates phase. The right panel is the live inverse FFT of your edited spectrum.

Try: attenuate the outer ring (low-pass blur) · attenuate the center (edge filter) · stamp a tiny faint dot far from center, then crank difference — that's a frequency-domain watermark · write text in the spectrum and see the grating texture it hides in the image · scribble on phase and watch the picture smear while its spectrum barely changes.
